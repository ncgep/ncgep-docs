# Privacy Domain (R&D)

NCGEP treats **Privacy** as a future **sovereign Domain** research track, not as a modification of Core.

As of 2026-08, Privacy is in **architecture R&D**. No public Privacy network exists. Detailed candidate designs and cryptographic comparisons remain internal until an architecture decision is approved.

Public commitments that already apply:

- no EVM requirement;
- no custom novel proving systems;
- no protocol-wide decryption backdoor;
- Dalet remains canonical on Core;
- IBC interoperability remains deferred until a compatible released pin exists.

For client integration with existing Domains, see [Client SDK](./CLIENT_SDK.md).

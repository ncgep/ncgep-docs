# Trust concepts

- **Chain consensus trust** is limited to that chain’s state machine.
- **Relayers** are not trusted to invent balances.
- **Manifests** are discovery metadata; verify provenance.
- **Official vs third-party** classification is a publication/policy signal for
  clients, not a cryptographic axiom.
- **Enterprise evidence/attestations** prove that an authorized actor recorded a
  claim — not that the underlying real-world fact is true.

Clients must visibly distinguish canonical Dalet/Diamonds from similarly named
third-party assets and vouchers (when IBC exists).
